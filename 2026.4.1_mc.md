# What's New in 2026.4.1

## What's new

- **Dates match your device region.** Dates throughout the app now follow the format of the region set on your device, including date pickers and list groupings. For example, a device set to the United Kingdom shows 29/09/2026 instead of 9/29/2026.
- **Update many rows at once.** Your administrator can now add a button that updates several rows in a form in one tap, such as marking every open task complete. Only matching rows change, and if anything goes wrong nothing is changed and you are told why.
- **Dashboards can be tailored.** Administrators can now hide individual dashboard widgets or rows for your app without an app update.

---

## Fixes and improvements

- Parts rows now display correctly when a part has no bin assigned, instead of showing an error.
- Lists no longer show technical error text when a row cannot be displayed. The row shows what it can, or a short "This item can't be displayed" note.
- Failing a task and adding the required comment now records both changes on the work order, so task history is complete.
- Searching a list no longer shows the previous search's results while the new ones are loading.
- On Android, pressing the back button on a form opened from the menu now opens the menu instead of leaving a blank gray screen.
- If camera access is turned off for the app, "Take photo" now explains what to do and offers to open Settings instead of doing nothing.
- General stability improvements.
