# What's New in 2026.4.1

## What's new

- **Smarter booking conversations.** Forms the assistant sends you can now show or hide questions based on your earlier answers, ask yes/no questions, and check email addresses and phone numbers before you submit. Your answers stay put if you scroll away and come back, and the assistant's replies arrive as one message instead of several.
- **Editing a proposed booking is quieter.** Tapping Edit on a booking card opens the form directly, and coming back no longer sends a message to the assistant.
- **Dates match your device region.** Dates throughout the app now follow the format of the region set on your device, including date pickers and list groupings. For example, a device set to the United Kingdom shows 29/09/2026 instead of 9/29/2026.
- **Dashboards can be tailored.** Administrators can now hide individual dashboard widgets or rows for your app without an app update.

---

## Fixes and improvements

- Sending a message no longer waits on your location. On the web, a slow location lookup could delay a reply by several minutes.
- Once a booking is made, older booking offers in the conversation are no longer tappable.
- Numbered options in the conversation stay on one line, even past 99.
- Booking screens show dates in your device's regional format.
- Lists no longer show technical error text when a row cannot be displayed. The row shows what it can, or a short "This item can't be displayed" note.
- Searching a list no longer shows the previous search's results while the new ones are loading.
- On Android, pressing the back button on a screen opened from the menu now opens the menu instead of leaving a blank gray screen.
- If camera access is turned off for the app, "Take photo" now explains what to do and offers to open Settings instead of doing nothing.
- General stability improvements.
